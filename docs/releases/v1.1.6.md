## Sounds and music: loops of each track

- **Loop of this track**: every track of a music has its own loop. Choose its start and end (typed, or from where the track is playing), shown on its waveform, and **Apply loop** repeats that part up to the end of the music, for example audio shorter than the music.
- The game loops the whole music at one point, every track together. **Loops without a jump** also moves the music's loop start so that its loop lasts whole repeats of the track's, and the track goes on without a jump.
- Without **Loop**, a track has no loop of its own: it plays to the end you choose (**Ends at**), then it is silent to the end of the music.
- **Loops without a jump** for the music's own loop: Apply moves the loop start a little (at most half a second) to where the end of the music goes on best into it. On the game's own music it finds the game's loop start.
- **Volume** and **Pan** for the whole music, under its loop: with one track they are that track's, with several they move every track by as much.
- **Apply loop** is shown only when there is a change to apply.

## Ghost preview in 3D

- **Hide timeline** leaves only the playback controls and the clock, for more of the track in view.
- The timeline can be made taller or shorter with the handle beside it (double-click: back to its height), also in the ghost editor. Both choices are remembered.
- The camera behind the kart no longer moves back and widens on every boost (mushroom or mini-turbo).
- The drift's sparks start at the rear wheel, where it touches the road; before, they appeared far behind the vehicle. The blue and orange mini-turbo sparks now shoot backwards and up, out of the road, and the smoke no longer covers them.

## Drop-down lists

- Every drop-down list of the editor now uses the same list as the settings (ghost editor, materials, collision, fog, primitives, texture format, track generator, vehicle and character parameters, menu resources, vertices, object appearance, sound properties).
