## Collision tab: every collision of the track, listed

- The Collision tab lists the track's collision the way the Track tab lists the track: by surface, then by variant, then by connected piece. Each line has its colour, its triangle count, an eye to show or hide it and a **+** to add more of it. **Find** searches the list, and **Show all** / **Hide all** switch every surface.
- Click a piece to select it (Shift adds, Ctrl toggles), double-click to go to it. Variants say what they do on this track where that is known, for example *Variant 3 · Underwater music* on Koopa Cape.
- **Add** at the top of the tab replaces *Create collision*: the shapes (and *Gate across the road* for Sound and Effect Triggers), with a **Surface** submenu for the kind of collision.
- **Settings of this collision**: the selected triangles show their surface, variant (with its meaning), shadow, **Trickable** and flag, and can be changed there for all of them at once. A trigger also says which way it is crossed: *Gate facing the karts*, *Gate for going back*, *Flat* or *Upright*.

## Collision in the documentation JSON

- The documentation JSON (Game files › Object documentation) can now describe collision too, in a `"collisions"` part beside `"objects"`: a label, description and notes for each surface, the meaning of its variants, and variants for a given track. Replace, Export, Edit and Restore default handle both parts; a file with only collisions keeps the objects' documentation.
- The editor documents Sound Trigger (with the variants of six of the game's tracks), Effect Trigger, Cannon Activator, Fall Boundary, Solid Fall, Boost Ramp, Boost Panel and Force Recalculation.

## Editable primitive models

- **Add › Primitive model (beta)…** opens the primitive window from the toolbar's Add button too.
- **Keep it editable** (off by default; its **?** explains it) keeps the shape's settings in a small file inside the `.szs` (`mkwii_editor_primitives.json`, which the game does not read). The shape is then named *Block 1*, *Cylinder 2*… in the Track tab, and **Edit this primitive…** in its Inspector opens the window again with its settings: **Apply** builds it again where it stands, replacing its model and its collision in one Ctrl+Z step.
- The collision of an editable shape follows it when the whole shape is moved, turned or resized.
- Changes made to the shape by hand (vertices, UVs, turning it) are not kept when it is built again from its settings.

## Ghost preview

- **←** and **→** go back and forward one frame, ten with Shift, in the ghost editor's preview and in the 3D window. They are left to a field, list or menu that has the focus.

## View on opening

- The 3D editor opens in **Model** again. Saving the Settings window while the Collision tab was open saved the tab's temporary Both as the view to open with. This no longer happens, and a Both saved that way goes back to Model once.

## Validation status

The automated tests pass, including new ones for the collision list and documentation and for editable primitives. In the editor, on copies of the game's tracks:

- On Koopa Cape the Collision tab listed every surface. A Sound Trigger gate was picked from the list, with its settings, documentation and facing shown, and its variant was changed and undone.
- On Luigi Circuit an editable block with wall collision was added, changed into a cylinder with road collision (the 432 old collision triangles removed, the 224 new ones added), moved with its collision, and undone back to the original track.
- The ghost editor's tests step through a staff ghost with the arrows, in the editor and in the 3D window.
- Opening with a saved Both opens in Model once; choosing Both afterwards is kept.

Editable primitives have not been tried in Dolphin or on a Wii yet.
