## Track check: crashes told apart, no errors on the game's own tracks

- The check now runs on all 51 of the game's own tracks and arenas without a single error. Battle arenas and the demo and mission scenes no longer get errors for missing checkpoints or item routes. Route-searching cameras (type 2) without a route, fixed cameras linking a route they do not use, and one-point routes are no longer errors either: the game's own tracks have them.
- Issues are now listed in sections in **Check** and in the save window: **Crashes or freezes the game** first, then errors that break races, warnings and notes. Problems that crash the game have their own icon, and the save window only says the track will crash when one of them is there.
- Arenas no longer show hundreds of warnings about their enemy route links, which are a network rather than a lap. Key checkpoint numbers that skip a value are now a note, as on some of the game's tracks.

## Saving

- The save window has **Don't show this again until the editor is closed**. With it, saving no longer stops for errors or warnings for the rest of the session.

## Cameras

- Fixed the editor's 3D view keeping a camera's field of view after leaving the cameras. The normal view returns when you leave the **Cameras** tab. After **Go to camera** elsewhere, it returns as soon as you move the view yourself.

## Sounds and music

- **Sound effects and voices** now lists every sound in `revo_kart.brsar`, not only the first 400. The list stays fast, however many there are.

## Discord activity

- More statuses: the open tool (effects, minimap, music and sounds, game text, cups, preview videos, textures, sculpting, a new track…), **Listening to** the music or sound being played, watching the intro or a ghost replay, painting collision and the 2D map. They also show from the home screen.

## Minimap (beta)

- The **In game** panel shows the finish line as a checkered bar across the road, as the game draws it over the map. It is drawn by the editor, not taken from the game's files, so its exact look may differ a little.

## Validation status

The automated tests pass, including new ones for the game's own track cases, the crash marking and the Discord statuses. The check was run on all 51 original tracks of the game with no errors. The field of view fix, the save window option, the Discord statuses and the minimap finish line were checked in the editor. The full sound list was not opened with a real `revo_kart.brsar`. These changes have not been verified on a Wii or in Dolphin.
