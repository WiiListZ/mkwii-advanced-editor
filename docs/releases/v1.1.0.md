## Ghosts: edit, record and watch them where they really drive

**Ghost editor** (Home, or *Tools › Ghost editor…*) opens your recorded ghosts, the game's staff ghosts, the ghosts of a Dolphin save and any `.rkg` file. The ghost that is open is highlighted in the list.

- **Details and inputs**: finish and lap times, track, character, vehicle, controller, drift type, date, and every input entry, with a frame-by-frame **Preview** of what the driver pressed (Space plays and pauses).
- **Compare** sets a ghost against another: lap times with the difference on each lap and since the start, and both ghosts' inputs along the race with where they differ.
- **Ghosts of this track** (Outline, or *Tools › Ghosts of this track…*) replaces the staff and expert ghosts of the track's position with a recorded ghost or a `.rkg` file, with a backup that **Restore** puts back.

### Record a ghost (beta)

*Tools › Record a ghost…* needs no track open. Drive a Time Trial in Dolphin with the window open; steps in the order of playing say what to do next.

- **Every run is kept**, not only the ones the game saves: the game keeps a run's ghost only when it is the track's best, so each finished run is also read from the game's memory as the race ends. It is the same file the game would have saved, checked byte for byte against one it saved.
- A run found both ways (saved by the game and read from memory) is one ghost, not two.
- Checked on Mario Kart Wii **PAL**. NTSC-U, NTSC-J and NTSC-K use the same addresses carried over with MKW-SP's port tables, not yet tried on those games; when the memory does not fit, only the save is used.

### Watch in 3D (beta): the path the game drives

A ghost file holds the buttons pressed, not positions. **Watch in 3D** now shows where the game really drives it:

- **Worked out with [Kinoko](https://github.com/vabold/Kinoko)**, a reimplementation of the game's physics bundled with the editor. With the game's `Race/Common.szs` and the track's file in the game folder, a ghost is driven in about a second, with no Dolphin. All 64 staff ghosts reach their own finish time on the game's tracks, as in the game.
- **On a different track** (one edited since the ghost was driven, or another track in that position) the preview says from which lap the path is no longer the game's.
- **Read the path from the game…** has the game itself race a ghost in Dolphin, in place of the track's staff ghosts, and reads its kart on every frame. The staff ghosts are put back afterwards, also if the editor was closed in between.
- **Recorded runs** keep the path their kart drove, read from Dolphin as the race ran.
- **Drift effects**: smoke at the rear wheels while drifting, blue sparks once a mini-turbo is charged and orange ones for a kart's super mini-turbo, and boost flames for mini-turbos, mushrooms and ramps, as the game had them.
- **The camera behind follows the way the kart goes**, eased, so drifts no longer swing it out to the side.
- Without the game's files the path is the earlier approximation, and the preview says so.

## Music with several tracks

*Tools › Sounds and music*: every music now has a **Tracks** section.

- **Each track** can be played alone, replaced on its own, exported, given its own **volume** and **pan**, or taken out. The preview places tracks as the game's sound engine does, and a volume or pan moved while the music plays is heard at once.
- **Add a track…** adds your own audio as a new track, fitted to the music's length, up to 4 tracks (8 channels), the most the game plays of one music. When a music goes beyond the room the game sets aside for it in `sound/revo_kart.brsar`, that room is made larger for that music only, in the same save.
- **When each track plays** is shown. Six course positions change tracks while a kart drives (Toad's Factory, Wario's Gold Mine, Koopa Cape, Dry Dry Ruins, Moonview Highway and Bowser's Castle): it happens on **Sound Trigger** collision, and the brush beside a variant paints it on the open track. Elsewhere all tracks play together, and the editor says so.
- **How it works** opens a new tutorial (*Help center › Tutorials › Music with several tracks*).
- Adding tracks is checked in the files the editor writes, not yet in the game: try it in Dolphin.

## Minimap editor (beta)

*Outline*, or *Tools › Minimap (beta)…*, edits `map_model.brres` like a drawing over the track:

- **Paint**, **Erase**, **Fill**, **Recolour** and **Stretch** (colours the road between two lines, in the direction of driving), with **Smooth edges**.
- **New from track…** builds a map from the road, boosts, off-road, finish line, objects, item boxes or enemy route; **Road only** does it in one click.
- **Frame** moves the map's frame, and the **In game** panel shows how the race will draw it. The side panel can be resized.
- **Minimap colour of the game** (*Tools*) sets the colour the game multiplies every minimap by, for all tracks.
- Rebuilt maps have been checked in the editor, not on a Wii.

## Fog, lighting and effects

- **Lighting, Fog and Effects** have rows in the Outline, near Sky; the Inspector shows what the track holds of each.
- **Fog of this track** edits the four fog settings of `posteffect.bfg` (kind, colour, start, end and fade) with a picture of the track under that fog, and lists and adds the areas that swap to each one.
- The **effects editor** can now duplicate and remove emitters.

## Track model

- **Materials** (*Tools › Materials of this track…*) changes how materials are drawn: faces shown, translucency and blending, alpha cut-out, depth, and material, TEV and constant colours.
- **Primitive models** (beta) adds blocks, ramps, cylinders, cones, spheres, rings, stairs and panels to the track model, with taper, twist, bend, lean, bulge and roughness, and optional collision.
- **Sculpting** (beta) raises, lowers, flattens or smooths the model with a brush; collision can follow the same strokes.
- Indexed palette textures (C4/C8/C14X2) can be replaced: they are reduced to their palette's colours.

## New track

- **Complete templates**: Grand Prix circuit, Mountain rally, Desert dunes, Snow valley and Castle crossing, each with its shape, look, road width, boost pads and scenery.
- **Shortcuts** (beta): draw where a shortcut leaves and rejoins the road; checkpoints and routes split into linked groups, and the walls open where it meets the road.
- **Scenery** places the game's own trees or flags beside the road, and **Smooth** rounds the curves of a layout already drawn.

## Checking, history and testing

- **Version history and compare** (*File*) keeps what a track held before each save (up to 20), lets you go back to one, and compares the open track with a version or another file: elements added, removed, moved or changed, marked on the track.
- **Validator** also checks the routes against the collision (routes through walls, checkpoints off the route, item boxes in walls, respawns over a fall or facing a wall) and shows the **Track size** against the largest original track and the formats' limits.
- **Open game in Dolphin** (*Tools*) starts the game from the extracted game folder, offering to save first.

## Object documentation

- The editor for the documentation JSON opens as a **form** (objects, descriptions, status, settings and their choices), with the **raw JSON** in a second tab. Both edit the same text and share one check.
- The raw JSON is **coloured**, and an error is marked on its line with its line and column; **Go there** now works in the editor's window too.
- The documentation card's buttons are laid out again: Sync beside the last sync, the JSON actions together, and **Restore default** only when your own JSON is in use.
- All **eight settings** are visible for every object, including empty ones. Field labels are bold, and the status and filter menus use the editor's dark theme.
- **Search objects** can be combined with filters for documentation status, percentage and settings coverage. **Filters** shows or hides the controls, with an active-filter count and **Reset filters**.

## Settings

- **Discord activity Status** is now in a dedicated **Discord** section in Settings, with the same Discord icon and switch.

## Included components

`ghost-path.exe`, built from [Kinoko](https://github.com/vabold/Kinoko) (MIT), works out ghost paths; it is unpacked to the editor's settings folder on first use and checked against its SHA-256. No game files are included. See `vendor/NOTICE.md`.

**Windows x64:** download `MKWiiAdvancedEditor.exe`. The accompanying `.sha256` file verifies the download and is also used by the in-app updater.
