## Critical hotfix — Editor window size, clicks and dialogs

> **Critical hotfix:** Fixes a severe window-sizing and startup-focus bug where the editor's WebView2 surface could start out of sync with the frameless window's dimensions or lose input focus at first launch, leaving buttons and menus unresponsive to clicks until the window was resized or maximized.

- **Editor window startup, dimensions and input focus**: strips the native window frame before sizing and showing the window, forwards `WM_SETFOCUS` and parent-position changes (`NotifyParentWindowPositionChanged`) to WebView2, isolates WebView2's profile folder per backend, and guards title-bar/edge drag against already-released clicks so the window never freezes in a modal move/size loop at startup.
- **New track** (`Ctrl+N`) now opens the track generator directly in the current window; only **Generate track** opens the finished track in its own 3D editor window, keeping any open document and unsaved work untouched.
- **File and folder dialogs stay in front of the editor**: Windows Open, Save and folder pickers (**File › Import › 3D model into track…**, **KMP (replace course data)…**, **KCL (replace collision)…**, **OBJ as collision…**, workspace and mod pickers) now always appear above the editor window instead of opening behind it.
- **In-app updater cleanup**: `MKWiiAdvancedEditor.exe.previous` and already-installed update files are now removed automatically once an update completes.

## 3D model import fixes

- **Static palette textures**: indexed BRRES textures (`CI4`, `CI8` and `CI14X2` with `PLT0` palettes, including custom mipmaps and multi-palette variants) are expanded losslessly before passing models to ABMatt, fixing import errors on paletted models. Models with animated texture palettes (`PAT0`) are rejected with a clear message instead of silently losing colours.
- **Materials, diffuse colours and vertex colours**: preserves constant diffuse/base colours across `OBJ` and Blender-converted models (`FBX`, `glTF`/`GLB`, `DAE`, `BLEND`), splits multi-material `OBJ` meshes per `usemtl` so the last material no longer overwrites earlier faces, and keeps `OBJ` vertex colours (`v x y z r g b`).
- **Accurate texture bindings**: isolates imported materials and keeps unique material and texture names across repeated imports so similarly named track or previously imported materials cannot swap textures or shaders. Source BRRES `TEX0` data is preserved directly without lossy `CMPR` re-encoding.
- **Broader OBJ support**: triangulates polygon faces (`n`-gons, including concave polygons), resolves negative vertex/UV/normal indices and imports faces that omit UVs (`v//vn`).
- **Animated models and multi-part placement**: fixes animated and `SHP0` import failures, and keeps all material parts of a static imported model aligned together when resting it on the chosen point on the track, with full **Undo**/**Redo** and SZS save support.

## Discord activity Status

- **Discord activity Status** is enabled by default at the top right of **Home**, beneath **Discord**, with live connection status.
- With the Discord desktop app open, your profile shows what you are doing in MKWii Advanced Editor, the track being edited and elapsed time, following the most recently focused editor window without ever sharing local folder paths.
- Turn the switch off to clear your Discord presence immediately; your choice is remembered between sessions.

## Cups and tracks

- **Play background videos** at the top right of **Tools › Cups and tracks…** lets you turn off the moving THP track previews behind cup rows immediately. Your preference is shared with **Video previews** in track lists and remembered between sessions.
