## Pulsar packs: menu videos

- **Menu videos (experimental, PAL games)**: a checkbox under *Keep original game cups* (and a question when creating a pack) turns the videos of the cup and course selection back on in Pulsar's engine (`Binaries/Code.pul`). Each original cup now shows its own video in the right order, on the cup page and on the track buttons, from the first time a page opens. The box of track names goes back over the video, where the game has it. No video file of the game is copied into the pack.
- **Single-player menu**: the Grand Prix, Time Trial, VS Race and Battle buttons play their videos again (Pulsar's extra Time Trial buttons use the Time Trial one).
- Packs made by an earlier version are recognised and fixed by **Apply changes…**; the original engine is kept as `Code.pul.original` and put back when the videos are turned off.

## Pulsar packs: custom cups' music and preview videos

- **Audio file…** in a custom track's music menu: WAV, MP3, Ogg, FLAC or BRSTM, converted into the track's own `CTBRSTM/<track>_n.brstm` and `_f.brstm`, with loop, volume and a faster final lap. **No music** writes a silent stream. Both follow the track when it moves. The pack's own music may take at most 500 MB: the size is shown when you choose a file, and a file that would go over is refused.
- **Its music from before**: a track whose own music was replaced by an apply (No music, an audio file, another track's music) gets it back from its `.bak` copy, which is now kept also when the music files are removed.
- **Preview videos for custom cups**: **Edit in the preview video editor…** opens the track's strip of its cup's video (`Assets/thp/course/pulsar_<n>.thp`, four strips of 400 × 144 as in the game's). A cup without one gets a black one the first time. At most 32 custom cups can have a video; beyond that, the option is greyed out with the reason. The cup selection stays black for custom cups (its one video holds the 8 original cups).
- The music and preview menus of custom tracks now look and work like those of the game's cups.
- **Crash on custom tracks fixed**: Pulsar shows cups in pairs, and a pack with an odd number of cups crashed when a custom track was chosen ("Invalid read from 0x00000008"). Apply adds a copy of the first cup to fill the pair.
- **Names**: names written by earlier versions without their font showed question marks in the game; Apply writes them again with the game's full font.
- **Open a mod** can now take the folder the mod is in (the game folder or the SD card with the riivolution folder).

## Sounds on the track

- **Add a sound here…** in the Inspector (on an object, a point or a model part) places one of the game's ambient sounds: river, big river, waterfall, big waterfall, lake, sea waves, fountain, volcano, audience or Mii audience. Each is an invisible emitter that plays its sound in a loop, heard as karts come near it. **Listen** plays its samples from `revo_kart.brsar` before you add it.
- The emitter gets a short route of its own: stretch it along a river or a shore to spread the sound.
- **Replace its sound…** on an emitter that is already on the track opens *Sounds and music* on the samples it plays. They are shared by every track (and by the sounds that use the same samples, such as the river and the big river), and the editor says so before opening them.

## Game cups

- **Change cup icon** on the game's race cups: a picture (PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, GIF) fitted into the game's own icons, keeping their size and format: the 128-pixel icon of the menus, race, awards and channel, and the 14-pixel one of the licence screen. It shows at once and is written with **Apply**; **Keep the current icon** forgets it.
- **No music** and **No preview video** for any position: the music becomes silent, and the track's part of the cup's video turns black.
- **Applied changes can be undone from the same menus**: **Original music of this position**, **Original preview video** and **Original icon** put the game's own back when you apply, from the `.bak` copies kept by the first apply. Before, after applying **No music**, choosing the track's music again left it silent. A music stream that differs from its `.bak` (changed by an earlier version or by hand) offers its original too.
- Choosing another track's music takes that track's original music, also when its own position was given **No music** or other music.
- The original cups inside a mod's view have the same rows, menus and **Apply** as in the game's cups; their changes go to the game folder, separately from the pack.
- The cups window is a little larger, so it no longer needs scrolling.

## Preview video editor

- **Video file…**: choose where the part starts and ends, and change it later in the part's panel.
- A custom cup's video lasts 6 s unless you choose its **Length of the preview** (1 to 8 s, as long as the game's videos of a cup's tracks). Every frame stays within the memory the game sets aside for these videos.
- Custom cups' videos of the open mod are listed at the top, with the open one in view.
- Changing a part's video no longer shows the old and the new one mixed for a moment.

## Track settings

- The music and preview video of the open track's place in the cups: **Music…** opens Sounds and music on its stream, **Preview video…** its part of the cup's video.

## New track (generator)

- Tricks only where the road throws the kart into the air (the end of a climb, a crest), not at every point of the road.
- The road is 3600 units wide by default (3200 before). Bends are eased to a radius of at least 1.4 road widths, and slopes to a 20% grade at most, with gentle crests.
- Item boxes stand on the road, as on Luigi Circuit, instead of floating above it.
- The road's painted edge no longer zigzags on bends.

## Game text

- The preview of a message finds its screen by itself in more cases: every page title (licences, options, Wi-Fi, friends, ghost data, the Mario Kart Channel…), every line of the instruction bar and every Yes/No question with its answers, found where the game's code uses them.
- Names added by mods open on the cup and course selection, in their cup and row next to the other names of the same mod: LE-CODE cups and tracks in `Common.bmg`, Pulsar cups and tracks in the pack's own file.
- A message whose screen is not known shows its text alone with the game's font, icons and colours, instead of a screen it may not be on. A screen you choose for a message is remembered (**Automatic** forgets it).
- Every message is listed, however many there are, with its colours and button icons as the game shows them. The text-only preview also draws the button icons, from the game's icon font.

## Other

- Notifications have an **X** to close them, and their buttons (such as **Show Riivolution XML**) work again, also while a window is open.
- **Show in Explorer** (and **Show Riivolution XML**) opens the folder window in front of the editor; before, it could open hidden.
- Pulsar packs whose `UIAssets.szs` has no `.` folder get their cup icons replaced instead of added a second time.

## Validation status

The automated tests pass, including new ones for the engine changes, the trick flag, the custom videos' table, the names' font, the pairing of cups and the ambient sounds (each finds its samples in the game's `revo_kart.brsar`, and they decode). Every Pulsar change was applied in the editor to copies of real packs and read back: engine state, custom music files, video files and their length, the track list's place. Taking back applied changes was run in the editor on copies of the game's files and of a pack: No music, then the original music (the same bytes as the game's), a black preview then the original part, a new cup icon then the original in all eight menu archives, and a custom track's own music after No music and after its removal. The menu videos of the cup and course selection (order, first opening, the box of track names) were checked in Dolphin. The single-player menu videos, the custom cups' videos and music, the ambient sounds placed on a track, the new cup icons, the generator changes and the pair crash fix have not been verified in Dolphin or on a Wii yet.
