## Effects in the 3D editor

- **Add › Effect…** (also **Add an effect here…** in the Inspector) lists the game's objects that are only a particle effect: embers, snow, falling leaves, a torch flame, volcano smoke, waterfalls, confetti, fireworks and camera flashes. Each says where its files come from: this track, or the track of the game folder that has them.
- Choosing one copies its effect files into the track when it does not have them yet, and places the object where you click on the track (or where the selected object or model part is). Move it, turn it and delete it like any object.
- **Edit this effect…** in the Inspector opens the effects editor on that object's effect.

## Sound Triggers: gates across the road

- Painting a **Sound Trigger** or an **Effect Trigger** now adds a gate where you click: an upright rectangle across the road, from wall to wall and from below the road to above a kart in the air. Karts drive through it, and the road stays road. Before, the brush turned the road's own triangles into the trigger, and karts could fall through the road there.
- The gate faces the karts, the way the enemy route goes. With **Going back: variant N** (on, variant 0), a second gate facing ahead goes just before it, so that karts driving back through it get back what they had before. This is how the game's own tracks place their Sound Triggers: upright one-sided gates, in pairs about 200 units apart.
- **Gate across the road** (on) can be unticked in the brush panel to paint triangles as before.
- A gate is ordinary collision: select it with **Select triangles** to move or delete it.

## Sounds and music

- The added tracks are listed without the main track, which is already the music above them. The music's **Volume** and **Pan** are the main track's; the other tracks have their own.
- The music says when its main track plays (on which Sound Trigger, as the added tracks do), with the brush beside each variant.
- A track's loop start typed or picked within a millisecond of the music's loop start is that loop start, so the two loops agree.

## Collision view

- Collision drawn over the model (Both) is drawn slightly in front of it, so it no longer flickers with the model's own triangles where they lie on the same plane.

## Validation status

The automated tests pass, including new ones for the gates and the effects list. In the editor, on copies of the game's tracks:

- On Luigi Circuit, embers were copied in from another track, placed, moved, opened in the effects editor and saved.
- On Koopa Cape, a Sound Trigger gate with variant 3 was added at the start, with the gate for going back (variant 0, 200 units before it, facing ahead). The road's triangles were unchanged, and the saved file holds both gates.
- The way the gates face and their order were compared with the Sound Triggers of every track of the game.

The gates and the effects have not been tried in Dolphin or on a Wii yet. The collision flicker could not be reproduced without a graphics card, so the fix was checked only by looking at the result.
